# Grant NOFO Analysis for $0-Revenue Entities

**Context:** When an entity (or two teaming entities) has $0 in prior federal grant/contract revenue, standard past-performance evaluation criteria become a liability. This reference captures strategies, pitfalls, and rules extracted from the SCALE Program (SBA-OIIGA-26-001) analysis session (July 22, 2026).

## The $0-Entity Reality

- **No organizational past performance** — the relevant scoring section relies entirely on individual key personnel qualifications.
- **No negotiated indirect cost rate** — cannot adopt another entity's NICRA (organization-specific per 2 CFR 200.414). Must elect 15% de minimis on MTDC.
- **No A-133 Single Audit** — must submit most recent financial statements instead. For a 2-month-old LLC, this means opening balance sheet, bank statements, capital contribution records — or a letter explaining none exist. Verify with the agency POC before submitting blank.
- **No conflict of interest policy** — must adopt a 2 CFR 2701.112-compliant policy before submission. Cannot promise post-award.
- **No payroll history** — cannot claim "actual benefit costs." Use statutory minimums (FICA 7.65%, FUTA 0.60%, SUTA at state new-employer rate) and cite BLS/state DOR sources.

## Teaming Strategy for Two $0 Entities

There are two valid approaches depending on the application context:

**Approach A — Sole Applicant with Key Personnel (preferred for grants):** One entity applies as 100% performer. The other entity's founder is named as key personnel on the prime's team. No subcontract is disclosed. The internal arrangement (revenue-sharing, product licensing, etc.) is a separate agreement between the founder and their own LLC — not visible in the application. Advantages: no 49% subcontract cap, simpler budget (all personnel under the prime), cleaner narrative. Use this when the application is small enough ($500K or less) that a formal subcontract doesn't add strategic value and the key personnel arrangement is credible.

**Approach B — Prime + Subcontract (required for large awards):** The entity with SDVOSB/DVOSB status primes. The other entity subcontracts. Max 49% subcontract cap (common in SBA grants): ensures prime self-performs majority of work. Budget for subcontract partner accordingly — 20-25% of total is safe. Use this when the award is large enough ($1M+) that a formal subcontract is expected, or when the funder requires disclosure of all team members.

**Individual credentials as mitigation:** Both entities' key personnel are the past performance. Each named person needs a verified, accurate resume (≤2 pages). Cross-reference against ALL available sources — internal dossiers, DFOP artifacts, LinkedIn, SAM.gov entity records. Credential contradictions (e.g., dossier says 4 years/84-person team; proposal resume says 15 years/11K personnel) are existential risks.

**Key verification questions for named personnel:**
1. Is this person's availability confirmed (employer permission, ethics approval, FTE commitment)?
2. Do ALL sources agree on their employment timeline, role titles, and scope of responsibility?
3. If they hold an existing federal grant (e.g., SBIR PI), does the combined FTE commitment exceed 100%?
4. Are their credentials verifiable from primary sources, not just internal dossiers?

## NICRA vs 15% De Minimis

Per 2 CFR 200.414(f):

- **NICRA:** Negotiated with cognizant agency. Takes months. Requires auditable cost allocation methodology and documented cost pools. Not feasible for tight deadlines.
- **15% de minimis:** Available to entities without a current negotiated rate (including provisional). Elected at any time, used indefinitely. No documentation required. Applied to Modified Total Direct Costs (MTDC).

**What MTDC excludes** (2 CFR 200.1):
- Portion of each subaward/subcontract OVER $25,000 (first $25K counts toward MTDC; anything above is excluded)
- Equipment over $5,000 per unit
- Tuition, participant support costs, capital expenditures, rental costs, and pass-through funds

**EO 14332 preference:** Gives preference to organizations with lower indirect costs. 15% de minimis is considered low enough — waiving indirect costs entirely provides no meaningful scoring advantage and leaves money on the table.

**Indirect Cost Statement template:**
> "[Entity Name] does not have a current federally negotiated indirect cost rate agreement (NICRA) and elects the de minimis rate of 15% of modified total direct costs (MTDC) pursuant to 2 CFR 200.414(f)."

## Personnel Budget Reconciliation

The most common error in $0-entity proposals:
- Narrative and budget MUST agree on personnel: same names, same FTEs, same salaries.
- Use a reconcilable table: Name, Role, FTE, Annual Rate @ FTE, Total Cost.
- Sum the cost column — that's your personnel total.
- If FTE × rate doesn't multiply to the cost column, the budget is wrong.

## Fringe Benefits for a $0 Entity

**DO NOT** claim "actual benefit costs" for an entity with no payroll history.

**Correct approach:** Statutory minimums + state-verified rate:
- FICA: 7.65% (mandatory Social Security + Medicare)
- FUTA: 0.60% (federal unemployment)
- SUTA: state new-employer rate (varies; SC ~0.80%, verify with state DOR/DEW)
- Workers' Comp: state-required premium (if any; needs quote)
- Total: approximately 9-10%

**Documentation:** Cite specific BLS tables and state publications in the budget narrative.

## SBA Risk Assessment (NOFO §5.3)

All recommended applicants undergo a risk assessment evaluating:
1. Record managing federal awards (zero — mitigate with individual experience)
2. Ability to implement statutory/regulatory requirements
3. Conflict of interest policy (must be 2 CFR 2701.112-compliant)

**Prepare before submission:**
- Draft a COI policy (procedures for employees/consultants not assisting clients with financial interest; not using project role to market outside services)
- Bank statements showing capitalization
- Insurance binders (if applicable)
- Document internal controls (segregation of duties, financial management system)

## NOFO Requirements Often Missed by First-Time Applicants

- **Font specification:** Often 12pt Times New Roman for narrative sections. Verify in NOFO.
- **Recruitment plan + geographic coverage:** NOFO explicitly requires estimate of businesses to serve, justification, recruitment channels, geographic scope.
- **Sustainability plan:** Must describe post-grant continuation. "Fee-for-service" needs specifics — offerings, price points, target market, revenue projections.
- **GrantSolutions.gov registration:** Separate from SAM/Grants.gov. Needed for post-award reporting.
- **SF-424A attachments:** Often requires specific attachment templates (A-9 through A-12 for budget detail worksheet). Use the exact named templates.

## Cross-Source Credential Verification

When research reveals conflicting personnel credentials across internal sources:
1. **Flag, don't choose.** Do not silently pick one version. Both appear in your research for a reason.
2. **Identify the provenance.** One source may be from a direct interview; another from a proposal artifact with embellishment. Note which is which.
3. **Verify with the individual.** Only the person themselves can resolve the contradiction.
4. **In the analysis, show both.** Present the contradiction openly with sources cited.
5. **Never circulate to external stakeholders** until the individual has confirmed their truthful, verifiable credentials.

**Common credential contradictions to watch for:**
- Military branch of service (USMC vs Navy)
- Employment tenure (4 years vs 15 years at same employer)
- Title scope ("Contract Compliance Manager, team of 84" vs "Senior Program Director, 11K personnel")
- Certification specialty (DAWIA III Program Management vs DAWIA III Contracting)
- Clearance level and status (active vs inactive/inactive-eligible)
