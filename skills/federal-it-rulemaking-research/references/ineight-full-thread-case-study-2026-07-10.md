## Lessons for Future Analyses

1. Always ask for the full thread — Amyn wasn't on the first week of emails (July 2-9). The thread revealed commitments that changed the entire risk picture.
2. When OCR'ing email screenshots, look for the CSP's commitments (LOA offers, timeline estimates, status updates) — these matter more than general discussion.
3. Distinguish "FedRAMP Ready" from "FedRAMP Moderate Equivalency" explicitly. Contractors and CSPs both conflate them.
4. When a stakeholder says "I have concerns about risk" (Rodney), produce a numbered risk register. This is the format they can take to a contracting officer.
5. Run adversarial review on every equivalency analysis. The fabricated quote error was caught by the judge, not the author.
