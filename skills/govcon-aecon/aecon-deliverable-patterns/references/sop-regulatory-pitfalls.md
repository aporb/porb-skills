# SOP Regulatory Pitfalls — Adversarial Review Findings

Source: Peer review of FCS Federal Procurement Manager SOP v1.0 (July 22, 2026)
Reviewer: Simulated federal procurement professional (15+ yrs DoD/DOE)

## 3 Material Factual Errors Found in v1.0

### 1. Small Business Set-Aside Threshold
- **Error:** SOP said "over the simplified acquisition threshold ($250K)"
- **Actual:** FAR 19.502-2(a) triggers at the micro-purchase threshold ($15,000)
- **Impact:** $15K-$250K procurements would miss the mandatory Rule of Two set-aside
- **Fix:** Use "above the micro-purchase threshold ($15,000 as of FY2026)"

### 2. Competition Standard
- **Error:** SOP presented "three bids in a buy" as a FAR requirement
- **Actual:** FAR Part 6 requires full and open competition with NO minimum bid count. FAR 13.106-3(b) suggests 3+ sources for simplified acquisitions only. FAR 13.202 requires no competition below $15K.
- **Fix:** Tiered standards: ≤$15K (none, FAR 13.202), $15K-$250K (3+ sources, FAR 13.106-3(b)), >$250K (full FAOC, FAR Part 6)

### 3. Section 889 Part B Missing
- **Error:** SOP only covered Part A (FAR 52.204-25, procurement ban)
- **Actual:** Part B (FAR 52.204-26) requires a separate representation about whether vendors USE covered telecom equipment. Must be flowed down to subcontractors.
- **Fix:** Add FAR 52.204-26 as a separate compliance checkpoint
