# Partner Charter Analysis — Session Reference (July 22, 2026)

## What Was Analyzed

LFC Charter v2.0 (Leatherneck Federal Consulting, LLC) — received from Douglas Henderson on July 21, 2026. Compared against HARBOR INITIATIVE LLC Charter v2.0 (the template sent) and the Bentree Rd 10 call transcript (47 min, July 21).

## Speaker Attribution — How Not to Get It Wrong

| Speaker | Talk % | Turns | Words | Actual Person |
|---------|--------|-------|-------|--------------|
| Speaker 0 | 23.4% | 77 | 1,719 | Douglas Henderson |
| Speaker 1 | 55.5% | 139 | 4,424 | Amyn Porbanderwala |

**Lesson:** In the initial analysis, "I had to pare back a ton of stuff" was attributed to Douglas. It was actually Amyn (Speaker 1, 55.5% of talk time). Always check the metadata stats before quoting. The person who speaks most is usually your principal, not the partner.

## STT Name Mangling

The transcript consistently transcribed "Leatherneck" as "Levenick" or "Levenich." This was a speech-to-text error — Douglas's pronunciation of "Leatherneck" with a drawn-out vowel caused the STT engine to mishear it. Never trust STT-transcribed proper nouns at face value; cross-reference against known entity names.

## Key Assumptions That Were Wrong

1. **"Douglas compressed his material"** — Wrong. Amyn compressed the Harbor charter to ~14 pages and sent it to Douglas. Douglas combined Amyn's compressed template with his own Leatherneck incorporation docs.
2. **"Aecon exit needed"** — Wrong. Everyone keeps their day jobs. This is the norm for GovCon partnerships.
3. **"SDVOSB-path is a confidence level"** — Partially wrong. The claim needs verification with Douglas on his VA rating, but the header vs. body inconsistency is a real issue.

## What the Analysis Missed Initially

- The "pared back" quote context: Amyn was describing his OWN process of compressing his Harbor charter to make it digestible for Douglas.
- Day-job norm: The partnership operates in parallel with primary employment. This should be the default assumption unless stated otherwise.
- Prior plan cross-reference: The partnership proposal (services-to-products flywheel) and the LFC charter (practitioner services firm) told fundamentally different stories about the business.
