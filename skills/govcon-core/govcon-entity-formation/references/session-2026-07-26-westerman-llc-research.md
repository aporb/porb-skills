This session was conducted for **Amyn Porbanderwala / HARBOR Initiative LLC** to research and recommend a masked/anonymous LLC structure for a subcontract with **Westerman Inc.** (nuclear fabrication, Bremen, OH).

**Context**: The contracting entity would be 100% owned by HARBOR Initiative LLC (Texas, UEI: K4CVRY71WQZ8, NAICS 541611). ## Key Findings

1. **Wyoming** is the best overall state for GovCon entities — anonymous formation, $104 filing, $60 annual report, strong asset protection case law, 0% state income tax, credible with primes
2. **New Mexico** is cheapest ($50 filing, $0 annual) but limited case law and banks/primes may flag no-annual-report entities
3. **Nevada must be avoided** — requires public manager listing on Annual List, defeating anonymity
4. **Delaware** is overkill at $300/yr franchise tax for a subcontract vehicle
5. **BOI/CTA reporting is moot** as of March 2025 — domestic entities exempt
6. **Login.gov admin identity proofing** is private (GSA-only, not public record)
7. **Disregarded entity** (default single-member LLC owned by parent LLC) is the right tax treatment for a subcontract vehicle — no separate tax return, QBI-eligible
8. **Northwest Registered Agent** at $125/yr is the recommended provider
