## The Software/Services Play (No Hardware Required)

Entities without drone hardware compete on the **orchestration, compliance, and support layer**:

1. **AI/ML Orchestration Software** — distributed AI for swarm coordination, target recognition, threat assessment. Runs on existing OEM platforms. Key: must work under SWaP-C constraints (<2 lbs compute payload).

2. **CMMC / NIST Compliance** — every hardware prime needs compliance subs for their C-UAS platforms. SSPs, POA&Ms, evidence packages. DFARS 7012 flow-downs.

3. **Program Management Support** — Replicator and DAWG are OTA programs moving fast. They need program managers who understand both the tech and the acquisition path.

4. **Training & Curriculum** — NAICS 611430. C-UAS operator training, CMMC awareness, AI/ML literacy for acquisition officers.
