## Verified Quotes

### Kirsten A. Davies, DoW CIO
- "In support of Secretary Pete Hegseth's directive to reduce compliance barriers for small and medium sized businesses, we are today suspending the CMMC Phase II requirements and initiating a 60-day study of the future of this program." (war.gov press release)
- "Robust cybersecurity and operational resilience remain critical to protecting American innovation and supporting warfighter readiness. We believe the DIB can achieve both, while we reduce unnecessary government red tape." (war.gov)
- "We are not reducing cybersecurity through this measure. We are reducing the red tape." (Breaking Defense press conference)
- "We're seeing that there's over 100,000 DIB businesses still that needed a third-party assessment conducted and somewhere in the neighborhood of 100, maybe a little over 100 assessors that are available for that. So the math just simply doesn't math for small to medium-sized businesses to even get compliant by the former transition date November 10, 2026." (Breaking Defense)

### Michael Duffey, Under Secretary of War for A&S
- "We have a strategic imperative to reduce bureaucracy as we build the world's strongest Arsenal of Freedom. The CIO's decision ensures we maintain a strict security baseline while removing paralyzing costs and keeping innovators and competition growing in the defense supply chain." (war.gov)
- "We're not relaxing any standards by any means. We expect businesses to adhere to the standards that NIST has outlined. What we're removing is the bureaucracy of the third-party assessment." (Breaking Defense)

### Kelly Loeffler, SBA Administrator
- "With over 100,000 small businesses impacted and compliance costs approaching as much as $600,000, the SBA strongly supports the Department of War's decisive action to preserve strong cybersecurity while cutting red tape." (sba.gov)
- CMMC compliance was "an untenable barrier pushing them out of the Defense Industrial Base." (sba.gov)

### Davies Memo (Core Argument Paragraph)
"The current iteration of the CMMC program, while intended to enhance security, imposes significant and often prohibitive burdens on the Defense Industrial Base (DIB), particularly the small and non-traditional businesses that are the engine of American innovation. While cybersecurity is essential, administrative compliance cannot come at the cost of warfighting capability and industrial base growth."

## What Changed vs. What Stayed

**Suspended (immediately):**
- CMMC Level 2 (C3PAO) — third-party certification assessments
- CMMC Level 3 (DIBCAC) — government-led certification assessments
- November 10, 2026 Phase 2 deadline
- All C3PAO/DIBCAC milestones in DoW solicitations and contracts
- Waiver procedures

**Still in force:**
- DFARS 252.204-7012 — Safeguarding CDI and Cyber Incident Reporting
- CMMC Level 1 (Self) — FCI self-assessment per FAR 52.204-21
- CMMC Level 2 (Self) — CUI self-assessment per NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2
- NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 — 110 controls
- DOJ Civil Cyber-Fraud Initiative — False Claims Act enforcement
- Select government-led assessments (DoW retains authority — not just self-assessment)
- Free DIB cybersecurity services: DoW DC3, NSA CCC, Project Spectrum

## ATS Pillars (from Duffey Memo)

3. Maximizing acquisition flexibility through reduced regulations and process ← CMMC suspension here
4. Developing high performance systems through rigorous enterprise technical execution excellence
5. Improving lifecycle risk management

## EO 14347 — Department of War Renaming

- Signed: September 5, 2025
- Legal status: Secondary title only — Congress has not changed statutory name
- "Department of Defense" remains the statutory name for legal purposes
- In practice: full adoption by Pentagon — war.gov, DoW CIO, "Secretary of War" used in all official materials
- Wikipedia now lists "also referred to as the Department of War (DoW)"
