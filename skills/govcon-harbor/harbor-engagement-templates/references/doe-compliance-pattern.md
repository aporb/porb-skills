# DOE/NNSA Vendor Compliance Requirements for Nuclear Fabrication Companies

From the July 2026 Westerman Inc. engagement. Research compiled from DOE O 471.1B, LANL Exhibit G, Pantex/Y-12 UCNI protocols, NRC regulations, and industry sources.

## 1. Cybersecurity Requirements (DOE O 471.1B / LANL Exhibit G)

**All DOE/NNSA sites** (LANL, Pantex, Y-12, SRS, Savannah River) impose NIST SP 800-171 compliance on vendors handling CUI/UCNI. Key requirements:

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Baseline standard | NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 (110 controls) |
| CUI/UCNI protection | Air-gapped or properly segmented systems for UCNI |
| Encryption | FIPS 140-2/3 validated cryptography for data-at-rest and in-transit |
| Mobile devices | Smartphones banned in UCNI processing areas |
| Incident reporting | 72-hour report to site CISO for any UCNI/CUI breach |
| Training | Biennial cybersecurity awareness + UCNI-specific handling training |
| Access control | Least privilege, MFA, visitor escort requirements |
| Media protection | Sanitization/disposal of UCNI media per NIST SP 800-88 |

**LANL Exhibit G Cyber Security Version 3.1** is the most detailed template — vendors must accept it as a flow-down.

## 2. Technology Control Plan (TCP)

Required when sharing export-controlled technology (UF6 cylinder designs, fabrication processes, metallurgy specs).

**TCP must include:**
- Physical security measures (access control, visitor logs, secure storage)
- IT security (air-gapped network, FIPS encryption, CUI handling procedures)
- Personnel screening (citizenship verification, export control briefings)
- Training (annual export control/deemed export training)
- Compliance assessment (annual self-inspection)
- Incident response for export control violations
- Recordkeeping (7-year retention per ITAR)

**Source:** NNSA Export Control Assistance Program (ECAP) provides templates.

## 3. Physical Security (10 CFR Part 73)

Facilities handling UF6 cylinders or related nuclear equipment must:

- Maintain a security organization with defined roles
- Implement physical barriers (fences, walls, locks on sensitive areas)
- Install alarm systems (intrusion detection, duress alarms)
- Conduct personnel screening and background checks
- Maintain visitor control and escort logs
- Keep 3 years of access records, 1 year of visitor logs
- Develop and maintain a Physical Security Plan (PSP)

## 4. Training Requirements

| Training | Frequency | Details |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| PX-6668 UCNI Training | Every 2 years | Pantex/Y-12 specific; covers UCNI identification, marking, handling |
| UCNI Briefing | Upon initial access | Covers 10 CFR 1016; includes non-disclosure agreement |
| Deemed Export Training | Annual | Covers EAR/ITAR, foreign national access controls |
| Cybersecurity Awareness | Annual | Site-specific policies, phishing, CUI handling |
| NQA-1 QA Training | Upon assignment | Quality assurance procedures for nuclear fabrication |

## 5. Export Control (10 CFR Part 110 and ITAR)

**10 CFR Part 110** — NRC export/import of nuclear equipment:
- Applies to UF6 cylinders specifically (designed for uranium hexafluoride)
- General license for most commercial shipments to certain destinations
- Specific license required for restricted destinations or sensitive end-uses
- Requires documented export control procedures

**ITAR considerations:**
- UF6 cylinder design data may be ITAR-controlled (US Munitions List Category VI or XIX)
- Technology transfer requires DSP-5 license for foreign nationals
- Deemed export rules apply (foreign national employee access = export)
- Expect TCP as flow-down from DOE prime

## 6. Quality Assurance (DOE O 414.1D)

DOE O 414.1D establishes quality assurance requirements for DOE nuclear facilities:

- **Applies to:** All work involving nuclear facilities, nuclear materials, or quality-related functions
- **Graded approach:** Requirements proportional to the hazard and importance
- **Must cover:** Design control, procurement, inspection, testing, corrective action, records management, audits
- **Criteria:** Based on ASME NQA-1-2008 and NQA-1a-2009 Addenda
- **Vendor/subcontractor flow-down:** QA requirements MUST flow to subcontractors via procurement documents
- **Records:** Quality assurance records must be maintained per DOE O 243.1

## 7. Combined Regulatory Burden

A nuclear fabrication company like Westerman must comply with **all** of these simultaneously:
- NIST SP 800-171 (CUI cybersecurity baseline)
- DOE O 471.1B (IT/cybersecurity for DOE sites)
- DOE O 414.1D (Quality assurance)
- 10 CFR Part 73 (Physical security)
- 10 CFR Part 110 (Export of nuclear equipment)
- 10 CFR Part 1016 (UCNI)
- ITAR/EAR (Export control)
- ASME NQA-1 (Quality assurance standard)

This creates significant overlap and potential conflict between requirements — a domain expertise differentiator for any consultant who can navigate them.
