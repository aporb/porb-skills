# 10-CLIN FFP SOW Structure — Westerman Engagement Pattern

From the July 2026 Westerman Inc. subcontract proposal. Copy and modify for new engagements.

## SOW Structure

### Header
- **Title:** Scope of Work — [Engagement Name]
- **Parties:** [Contracting Entity LLC] and [Counterparty Legal Name]
- **Effective Date:** [Date]
- **Contract Type:** Firm-Fixed Price (FFP)

### Section 1 — Background
1-2 paragraphs establishing: what the counterparty does, what compliance regime they operate under, why they need this engagement, and how HARBOR's expertise fits.

### Section 3 — Period of Performance
- Start: [Date or "upon execution by both parties"]
- End: [Date or N months from start]
- Optional: renewable for [N] additional periods

### Section 4 — Payment Terms
- **FFP** — no time and materials
- **Invoicing:** milestone-triggered per CLIN or percentage completion per phase
- **Net 30** from receipt of correct invoice
- **Retainage:** optional 10% held until final deliverable acceptance
- **Travel:** included in FFP (or T&M with prior approval if significant travel expected)

### Section 5 — Assumptions (Critical — Price Depends On)
List what the price assumes. If any assumption is false, price must be renegotiated. Examples:
- Counterparty provides reasonable access to personnel, facilities, and systems
- Counterparty maintains existing IT infrastructure as-is during assessment
- No material change in regulatory framework during PoP
- [N] designated counterparty POC with [N] hours/week availability
- Travel within [radius] miles of [location] is included; beyond requires prior approval

### Section 6 — Exclusions
List what is NOT in scope. Examples:
- Implementation of tool purchases (licenses, hardware)
- Physical security upgrades or construction
- Legal review of export control classification
- M&A due diligence or ESOP valuation
- Staff augmentation beyond training delivery
- Work requiring a U.S. Government security clearance (if not held)

### Section 7 — General Provisions
- **Governing Law:** Texas (or counterparty's state)
- **IP Ownership:** HARBOR retains methodology and pre-existing IP; engagement-specific artifacts assigned to counterparty upon full payment
- **Data Rights:** Counterparty owns all data collected during the engagement; HARBOR may retain copies for regulatory compliance
- **Confidentiality:** Standard mutual NDA governs (see template)
- **Export Control:** Both parties comply with ITAR, EAR, and DOE export control requirements
- **Independent Contractor:** HARBOR is not an employee, joint venturer, or partner

## Acceptance Criteria Pattern

Each CLIN deliverable should specify objective, binary acceptance criteria:

- **Document delivered:** File received by counterparty PM in agreed format
- **Content reviewed:** Counterparty provides written acceptance or specific revision requests within 10 business days
- **Training completed:** Attendance sign-in sheets
- **Deployment verified:** Technical acceptance test signed off by counterparty IT lead
- **Compliance artifact:** Importable/usable in target platform (SPRS, GCC High, etc.)
