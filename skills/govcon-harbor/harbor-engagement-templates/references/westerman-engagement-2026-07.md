## Errors Caught by Adversarial Review

1. **"Centrus — your acquirer" was FALSE.** Worthington Industries acquired Westerman in 2012. Centrus is a supply chain customer.
2. **ITAR penalty $1,448,000 under 22 CFR 126.13 was WRONG.** Correct: $1,271,078 under §127.10(a)(1)(i).
3. **"88-95% AI pilots never reach production (MIT/McKinsey)" was UNVERIFIABLE.** Gartner has related stats (70-87%) but the specific range + attribution was fabricated.
4. **CLIN 0001/0002 pricing drift.** Subagent independently inflated prices from $15K to $40-50K / $25-35K. Caught during trace review.
