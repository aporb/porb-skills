# Masked LLC Formation for HARBOR Subsidiary Contracting Entities

From the July 2026 Westerman Inc. engagement. Use when HARBOR needs a separate contracting entity (e.g., when Amyn has a concurrent role at another firm and needs name separation from public records).

## Recommended Structure

```
HARBOR Initiative LLC (Texas, UEI: K4CVRY71WQZ8, NAICS 541611)
    │
    └── 100% owns --> [Contracting Entity] LLC (Wyoming)
                        • Single-member, manager-managed
                        • Disregarded entity for tax purposes
                        • Own EIN, bank account, SAM registration
```

## Formation Steps

### Step 1: Northwest Registered Agent
- Website: northwestregisteredagent.com
- Select "Form a Wyoming LLC"
- Provide: desired LLC name, HARBOR as sole member
- Northwest files Articles of Organization with Wyoming SOS
- Cost: ~$229 ($104 state fee + $125 registered agent)
- Northwest provides their Wyoming address as principal office (critical for anonymity)
- **Articles list:** LLC name, registered agent (Northwest), RA's Wyoming address, organizer (Northwest) — NO member/manager names

### Step 2: EIN
- IRS Form SS-4 online at irs.gov — free, immediate
- EIN assigned to the LLC, not to Amyn personally
- Northwest can facilitate this

### Step 3: Operating Agreement (Attorney Recommended)
- HARBOR Initiative LLC as sole member
- Manager-managed (Amyn as Manager)
- Capital contribution clause
- Tax classification: Disregarded entity
- Indemnification and veil protection clauses
- Books/records separation requirements
- Cost: $500-$1,500

### Step 4: Organizational Documents
- Statement of Organizer (resigns organizer, confirms HARBOR as member)
- Initial Consent of Member (HARBOR adopts OA, appoints manager, authorizes bank account)

### Step 5: Bank Account
- Contracting Entity name + EIN
- Articles, OA, EIN letter, ID of authorized signer
- Recommended: Mercury (online, GovCon-friendly) or Chase
- **Critical:** Never commingle with HARBOR's accounts

### Step 6: SAM.gov Registration
- Create Login.gov account (Amyn as admin — NOT public)
- Register entity: legal name, WY address (RA address), EIN, NAICS 541611
- Wait 10-15 business days for validation
- Receive UEI and CAGE code
- **Note:** Beneficial ownership is NOT publicly displayed in SAM.gov

### Step 7: Certificates
- Certificate of Good Standing (Wyoming SOS — $10)
- EIN verification letter (IRS)

## SAM.gov for Anonymous LLCs

**Yes, an anonymous LLC can register in SAM.gov.** The anonymity is at the state public record level. SAM.gov requires legal name, physical address (RA address is acceptable), EIN, and NAICS. Beneficial ownership is not publicly displayed.

**Key timeline:** Formation is the fastest part (~8 hours). The bottleneck is SAM.gov validation (10-15 business days). Start SAM process immediately after formation. Total time to operational: ~4 weeks.

## Corporate Veil Protection

- **Separate bank account** — never commingle
- **Separate books** — QuickBooks/Xero for Contracting Entity only
- **Contracts in LLC name** — signed "[Entity] LLC, by Manager"
- **Capital contributions documented** — HARBOR pays expenses via documented contributions
- **Annual minutes** — document major decisions
- **Separate insurance** — PL, cyber, GL in Contracting Entity name

## CTA/BOI Update

As of March 2025, FinCEN revised the BOI rule. **Domestic U.S. entities are now EXEMPT from BOI reporting.** The CTA does not undermine Wyoming LLC anonymity for U.S. persons.
