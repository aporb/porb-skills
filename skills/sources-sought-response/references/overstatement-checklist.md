## Platform Depth Tiers (for capability statements)

| Tier | Label | Example | Evidence Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hands-on** | "Hands-on experience with X" | Advana, Navy ERP, M365 GCC High | Resume explicitly lists platform; work history confirms deployment |
| **Reference knowledge** | "Familiar with X" or "Published guide covering X" | Databricks, Palantir, Qlik | Covered in FDSH handbook but no personal deployment confirmed |
| **Awareness** | "Understanding of X requirements" | VA 6500, Section 508, FedRAMP | CISA cert, federal experience, or documented study |

Never use "Hands-on" language for Reference-knowledge or Awareness-tier platforms.
